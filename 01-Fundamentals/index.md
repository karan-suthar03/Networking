# Fundamentals of Networking

## What is this section about?

This section covers the **core concepts** that form the foundation of all networking:

- **Network Types** - Public internet vs private intranets
- **Network Components** - Nodes (devices) and Links (connections)
- **Data Units** - How information is divided into packets for transmission

Understanding these fundamentals is essential before diving into protocols, models, or advanced networking topics.

---

## Why does this matter?

- **Internet vs Intranet** → Understand the difference between public and private networks, security implications, and use cases
- **Nodes and Links** → Learn what constitutes a network at the physical level and how devices communicate
- **Packets** → Grasp how data is transmitted efficiently across networks, including error handling and reassembly
